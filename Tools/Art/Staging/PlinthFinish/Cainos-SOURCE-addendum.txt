2026-10-09 native stone paving variation:
The 32 StonePaving sprites derive from the existing individual Cainos Paving
00..31 PNGs (original TX Tileset Grass.png, 16x16 crops at x=0..63/y=128..255).
Exactly these six source grass colors were made fully transparent:
RGB (114,117,27), (120,123,33), (101,108,23), (127,130,38),
(104,111,22), and (127,129,36).
Every remaining stone pixel keeps its original RGBA value and 16x16 coordinate.
No resizing, repainting, palette replacement or generated art was used.
Custom center pivot (8,8), 32 PPU, Point filtering, no mipmaps/compression.
The originals remain unchanged. Per-PNG original/adopted hashes, retained and
removed pixel counts are in Tools/Art/Staging/PlinthFinish/prepared.json.
Extraction/import review is in ArtDirection/Review-20261009/plinth-finish/.
The original Cainos permission for modification applies; these variations are
included only as part of the compiled game, never as standalone asset packs.
