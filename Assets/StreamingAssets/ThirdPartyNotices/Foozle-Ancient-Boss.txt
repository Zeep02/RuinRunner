Ruin Runner uses 192 losslessly separated native cells on a common 96x96 canvas across four
directions: idle 7, walk 8, hurt 4, death 9, attack02 13, attack03 7 per direction.
80px source cells receive 8px transparent padding per edge; 96px side walk cells stay intact. All original pixels are preserved. Pixels are not repainted, rescaled or interpolated. Runtime timing follows
the existing boss warning, active and recovery phases. Source holds are
preserved and are not claimed as additional authored poses.
