The game applies a constant loudness adjustment to each original stereo WAV.
Forest Whisper also receives a 5 ms edge de-click at each loop boundary.
Original duration, tempo, pitch, arrangement and stereo are preserved.
The source archives, original files and exact hashes are retained in the project.

Terraria and DM DOKURO's sanctuary were references for the desired mood only.
Their recordings, melodies and samples are not included in Ruin Runner.
